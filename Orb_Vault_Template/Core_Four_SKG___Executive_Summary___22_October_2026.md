# CORE FOUR SKG

## Executive Summary \| Proof of Presence System / ORB \| 22 October 2026

# Executive overview

The Core Four Semantic Knowledge Graph (SKG) is an owner-approved design for making ORB’s reasoning, factual statements, ethical conduct, and knowledge maintenance more traceable. Four philosophical lenses—Locke, Hume, Kant, and Spinoza—independently examine structured evidence. Six pairwise cross-examinations and an advisory Fifth Mind expose agreements, disagreements, and unresolved questions. A versioned deterministic controller alone authorizes consequential decisions; A.I.M.S. remains the authoritative memory system.  
The architectural specifications are approved, not a demonstrated production implementation. The next phase must turn the rules and review records into executable predicates, testable controls, and evaluated behavior.

# The four lenses

Locke — Evidence provenance, independent corroboration, consent, and supersession.  
Hume — Proportioned confidence, causal inference, the is–ought distinction, and sympathy. Distress may hold discretionary sales progression—not the visitor’s requested task.  
Kant — Truthfulness, material alternatives, people as ends, and informed choice.  
Spinoza — Whole-system effects, consistency, evidence appropriate to stakes, truthful urgency, and qualified learning promotion.

# Governance and operational design

Equal standing: no philosopher has permanent priority. Operational authorization gates may take effect before wording decisions without ranking the lenses.  
Advisory synthesis: the Fifth Mind preserves minority objections and never executes actions. Unresolved ethical conflicts remain unresolved; silence never counts as approval.  
Efficient processing: fast deterministic checks run on relevant turns; full four-lens review is triggered by material events, not every conversation.  
Auditability: a frozen evidence manifest, atom-state hash, real seed seals, evaluation time, rule references, four verdicts, six cross-examinations, synthesis, controller decision, and A.I.M.S. event links support review and replay.  
Memory integrity: changes require an authorized controller decision and traceable review ID. Recovery must revalidate current state and prevent duplicate writes.

# Approved review and freshness policy

An unresolved material evidentiary objection holds retirement, creates a deduplicated evidence request, and registers re-review when relevant evidence arrives. Preserving an atom in A.I.M.S. is distinct from allowing it to be served as an eligible current fact; disputed or excluded retrieval status may apply independently.  
Maintenance uses incremental change-triggered reviews, time-based revalidation, and bounded periodic reconciliation. Vault confidence does not automatically decay with time; freshness must therefore be tracked separately. Approved configurable thresholds: prices 24 hours; business hours 24 hours; policies 7 days; terms and conditions 7 days. Consequential transaction terms are rechecked at commitment time.

# Executive conclusion

Core Four provides a coherent specification for accountable reasoning and supervised knowledge improvement—not yet proof of superior performance. Its value will depend on the quality of structured evidence, strict authority separation, reproducible audit trails, and measured outcomes in realistic evaluations.
